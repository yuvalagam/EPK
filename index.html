Professional Biography

Yuval Agam is an NYC/Boston-based multi-instrumentalist and educator who brings versatility, energy, and professionalism to weddings, corporate events, private parties, cocktail hours, showers, and other special occasions. He has performed throughout the Tri-State area and New England, working with event companies and entertainment groups including The Dukes of Circuit Ave, Sugarbabies Inc, Black Diamond Event Production, SpeechLess Music, Night Shift Entertainment, Summertone Productions, and others.

Yuval performs on saxophone, clarinet, and flute, and is comfortable playing a wide range of styles and settings. His performance credits include the Dani & Debora Gurgel Quartet, Mansour, and Voodoo Baby Aliens.

Available for independent bookings throughout NYC, Boston, and beyond.
